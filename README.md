## What is control-z?

control-z collects free post-production finishing tools for DaVinci Resolve in one shared suite and download location. The project targets the practical jobs that often appear near the end of an edit, giving creators accessible options for cleanup, enhancement, and final-stage work without requiring a collection of disconnected workflows.

Its toolset covers a broad range of finishing needs. Depending on the task, users can work with denoise-focused tools, transcription and captions, intelligent reframing, AI roto mattes, dialogue recovery, super-resolution, depth mattes, and Fusion templates. The centralized approach is intended for editors and finishing artists already working within a Resolve-based pipeline.
